## What this shows

Before the fix, any node run longer than about 255 steps reported its own honest evidence chain as broken,
and the live dashboard displayed `verify_chain BROKEN`. A guard that fires on honest data trains its reader
to ignore it. After the fix the retained window verifies, and the three registered tamper cases inside it
are still caught.
