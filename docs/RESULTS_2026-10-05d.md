## What this shows

- **Implementation:** `check_fresh` refuses old, equal, non-finite and non-numeric times, does not touch the
  nonce store when it refuses, and leaves K8's replay reason in place.
- **The price of a strict mark (P3):** with shuffled delivery, 988 of 1000 genuine records were lost. A strict
  high-water mark is only usable where records arrive in order.
- **The skew limit depends on the verifier's clock (P4 and the diagnostic):** with a stale `now`, legitimate
  records count as future-dated and are refused. Without the limit, one future-dated record locks the key out.
  Either way, freshness needs a verifier clock that is roughly right. Nothing here models that clock.
